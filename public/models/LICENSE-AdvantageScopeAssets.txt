The 3D field model public/models/field-2026.glb is derived from Field3d_2026FRCFieldV1 in
https://github.com/Mechanical-Advantage/AdvantageScopeAssets (release archive-v1), processed by
scripts/build-field-asset.ts (fuel removed, meshes merged and simplified, materials adjusted,
meshopt-compressed). The field design is FIRST's (FRC 2026 REBUILT playing field).
